# Cryogenic Multiaxis Laser Interferometer Data Repository

Data generated during the course of research for the article:

> A. Christiansen, D. Naylor, and B. Gom.
> "A cryogenic multiaxis sinusoidal frequency modulation range-resolved laser interferometer for far-infrared space-based astronomical instrumentation".
> Measurement Science and Technology, 2026. DOI: [10.1088/1361-6501/ae411f](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6501/ae411f).

## [`figure2.txt`](./data/figure2.txt)

Data used for Figure 2.

- `time`: The time as a fraction of one modulation period.
- `theta`: The phase modulation of Figure 2(a) in radians.
- `u`: The normalized interference signal of Figure 2(b).
- `w`: The window function of Figure 2(c).
- `c-inband-real`: The real component of the in-band carrier of Figure 2(d).
- `c-inband-imaginary`: The imaginary component of the in-band carrier of Figure 2(d).
- `c-outofband-real`: The real component of the out-of-band carrier of Figure 2(g).
- `c-outofband-imaginary`: The imaginary component of the out-of-band carrier of Figure 2(g).
- `d-inband-real`: The real component of the in-band demodulated signal of Figure 2(e).
- `d-inband-imaginary`: The imaginary component of the in-band demodulated signal of Figure 2(e).
- `d-outofband-real`: The real component of the out-of-band demodulated signal of Figure 2(h).
- `d-outofband-imaginary`: The imaginary component of the out-of-band demodulated signal of Figure 2(h).
- `q-inband-real`: The real component of the in-band quadrature signal of Figure 2(f).
- `q-inband-imaginary`: The imaginary component of the in-band quadrature signal of Figure 2(f).
- `q-outofband-real`: The real component of the out-of-band quadrature signal of Figure 2(i).
- `q-outofband-imaginary`: The imaginary component of the out-of-band quadrature signal of Figure 2(i).
